THE ART AND SCIENCE OF PROMPTING

Give Copilot the ingredients for a useful response

You don't need a perfect prompt. Start with the outcome you need, then add the details that make the response useful.

Goal

What do you want Copilot to do?

State the task clearly: summarize, compare, analyze, draft, or recommend.

Context

Why, and who is involved?

Add the customer situation, audience, priorities, and constraints.

Source

What should Copilot use?

Point to the relevant file, email thread, meeting, workbook, or web source.

Expectations

What should the result look like?

Specify format, tone, length, evidence, and the next action you need.

Keep the conversation going. Review the response, challenge assumptions, add missing context, and ask Copilot to improve the result.
